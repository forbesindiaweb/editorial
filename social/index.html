⚠️ Important: CORS & browser limits

Most social media APIs block direct browser requests due to CORS restrictions. This tool will attempt direct calls — which works for some APIs (LinkedIn, Threads) but may fail for others (X, Facebook, Instagram).

For full functionality: Deploy a small Google Apps Script proxy (one function per platform) and enter its URL below. This is the same pattern as your other Forbes India tools.
