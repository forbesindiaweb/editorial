DINK

Dear reader,

You must have heard of them. Double income, no kids (DINK) couples. Or maybe you are one of them. Or perhaps you haven’t decided yet.

Wherever you may lie on this, the truth is rising incomes, career-first mindsets and the sheer cost of raising children in urban India are nudging couples toward the DINK lifestyle, deliberately or by default. But zoom out, and it’s a visible symptom of a starker demographic shift.

Indians are having fewer kids than before and with that India’s fertility rate has dropped below replacement level. In a way, a country that once feared overpopulation is now beginning to reckon with its demographic mirror image.

So, this week, we go beyond the DINK conversation itself, speaking to demographers, economists, medical experts and DINK couples (of course!) to figure out how India should reimagine its economy.

And if you would rather read what led to N Chandrasekaran’s exit from Tata Sons and how the behemoth changed under him, we have you covered. But beyond the Tata group itself, India’s corporate boardrooms are quietly getting younger, with directors aged 26-45 gaining ground.

In our multimedia feature this week, the FILA Leadership Circuit, Chandigarh spotlights entrepreneurs building major brands beyond India’s metro hubs.

Until next time!
-Samreen
