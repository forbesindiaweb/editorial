👚One size doesn’t fit all and that’s changing

The Ministry of Textiles is trying to address the discrepancy in sizing of garments across different brands in India by creating a standardised national apparel sizing framework. The “INDIAsize” is based on an anthropometric survey undertaken by NIFT under the ministry, which involved taking full-body 3D body scans of over 26,000 Indians over 15 and above, producing 27 body-size charts, 14 for men and 13 for women.

📉NSE debuts to a tepid public market reception

The National Stock Exchange debuted on BSE on September 24 at a mere 1 percent premium over its issue price. The exchange’s IPO was the second largest for the Indian public markets at Rs 22,568.94 crore. According to securities analysts, NSE’s expansion across asset classes and financial products may support further business development.

➤NSE has been the largest stock exchange in India in terms of total turnover in cash market and total turnover in equity derivates from FY 2001 to FY 2026.

➤The exchange’s IPO plans have not been without challenges, ranging from regulatory concerns and governance concerns stemming from a co-location scandal since its first attempt at a public listing in 2016, which it had to pay out to settle.

➤NSE relies heavily on derivatives revenues at a time when regulators are looking to curb excessive speculation derivatives.

🏆Marathi film Gondhal’s pitch for the Oscars

India’s official entry to the 99th Academy Awards is the Marathi film Gondhal is writer and director Santosh Davakhar’s first feature film. A construction business owner, Davakhar took a screenplay-writing course and studied international cinema to realise his ambition to make cinema.

➤Based in the backdrop of Maharashtra’s traditional Gondhal ritual, the movie was made in a total budget of Rs 5 crores with barely any money spent on hiring popular actors.

➤Davakhar wanted to take the film to audience beyond Maharashtra and has dubbed it in five Indian languages including Hindi, with plans of dubbing in English.

➤Towards the campaign for the Oscars and to garner enough votes, the movie will be screened in New York next month, in addition to a budget set aside to promote the film.

🎱Should India prepare for fresh sanctions from the US?

US President Donald Trump’s move to signing into law the authority to impose up to 100 percent tariffs on countries buying oil and gas from Russia, is bad news for India. The law covers tariffs on all goods imported to the US from these countries – impacting exports from India spanning electrical machinery and equipment, pharmaceuticals, machinery, chemicals, textiles, gems and jewellery, and engineering goods.
