📈SBI Funds Management IPO is a good bargain say experts

SBI Funds Management (SBI Mutual Funds) which is set to debut on the exchange tomorrow with a final issue price of Rs 574 per share is already commanding an 18 percent premium in the grey markets, according to reports. The Rs 9,813 crore IPO was valued lower compared to listed peers believe experts, offering a rare opportunity to investors.

➤SBI Funds Management issue was available at a discount to peers like ICICI Prudential AMC and HDFC AMC at 38.1 price-to-earnings ratio.

➤SBI MF commands a market share of 15.3 percent, with strong EBITDA margins of 79 percent.

➤However, the MF business is concentrated in a limited number of schemes with top 10 schemes contributing to 46.5 percent of MF revenue in FY26.

📲Smartphones package old wine in new bottle for a premium

Smartphone makers have found a workaround to combat rising chip prices and the supply chain disruption caused by the ongoing war in West Asia. Instead of new device launches with advanced features, most brands are repackaging old variants with minor cosmetic differences like increased screen size or memory, at a higher mark-up.

➤Similar phones are being sold online and offline at a huge price variation to combat margin pressure, and compounding chipset prices.

➤Reusing existing hardware platforms significantly reduces R&D, testing, certification, and supply chain costs, believe industry experts.

➤However, the repackaged models have few takers as consumers walking in to test the devices at offline stores are discerning.

🤵Services economy production in hospitality clocks double-digit growth in April

India’s first Index of Service Production (ISP) to capture the services economy indicated strong growth in key sectors for the month of April 2026, topped by accommodation and food. The index released by The Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation (MOSPI) is a first-of-its-kind approach to gauge the formal services economy monthly.

➤Built on the base year of 2024-25, the index gauges the formal services economy which accounts for more than 30 percent of employment.

➤Accommodation and food registered a growth of 37.2 percent year-on-year, followed by retail trade at 30.8 percent, and administrative and support services at 28.7 percent.

➤Among the 19 sub-sectors being tracked, air transport contracted by 13.9 percent year-on-year due to the West Asia conflict.
