In a tumultuous week marred by calamities and deaths, one thing stood out – Meta committing to pay $18 billion over the next decade to a group of US states, territories and districts, for making its social media apps addictive for youngsters. In addition, Meta has agreed to implement safeguards to protect underage users of its social media apps, including WhatsApp, Instagram, and Facebook.

The proposed changes by Meta to ensure safety of underage users includes:

➤Two-hour daily time limit

➤Turning off access to its apps at night by default

➤Zero notifications during school hours

➤Notifications every 15 minutes of continuous screen time to take a break

➤New parental supervision controls

It has also called upon other social media companies like TikTok and YouTube, vide an open letter to join its ‘commitment’—but only in the US.

However, this could set a precedent for the rest of the world, with Australia banning children under the age of 16 from making accounts on social media apps like X, Facebook, TikTok, Snapchat, YouTube, Instagram, Threads, Reddit and some streaming platforms like Twitch and Kick.

While concerns around implementation of age-gating are legitimate, it is a step towards reducing screen time, and the mental health impact and risks minors are exposed to online.

In India, which accounts for the largest active user base for all of Meta’s apps globally, Karnataka and Andhra Pradesh have spoken about regulating the use of social media by children under the age of 16 and 13 years, respectively.

However, the proactive implementation of features by social media companies to protect young users online is still far away.

🍜Maharashtra FDA Commissioner Tukaram Mundhe’s mission

Majarashtra’s Food and Drug Administration Commissioner, IAS Officer Tukaram Mundhe has become a household name for his strict enforcement of food safety rules across food establishments of varying sizes and popularity. Mundhe’s objective has been to implement the Food Safety and Standard Rules 2011 in letter and spirit.

➤Over the last two months, Maharashtra FDA has conducted 3000 inspections across restaurants, cloud kitchens, quick commerce warehouses and dairies.

➤As part of its mission, the FDA follows an E3 strategy – empowerment of people, enabling businesses and enforcement of law.

➤Mundhe highlights the role of HFS (high fat and sugar) products resulting in rise in non-communicable diseases in India.

💲Early close to FCNR(B) dollar window by RBI

The central bank’s decision to advance the deadline to close the foreign currency non-resident (bank) or FCNR(B) deposits by a month has led banks scrambling to adjust their funding plans. The move indicates RBI’s confidence in its buffers.

➤FCNR(B) allows NRIs to invest their foreign earnings in a fixed deposit account in India at attractive interest rates, ensuring both principal and interest are fully repatriable.

➤In June, a series of measures was announced by the government to ease pressure on the rupee by attracting foreign capital inflows including FCNR(B) deposits from June 2026 to September 30, 2026.

➤With the advanced deadline of August 31, 2026, experts estimate FCNR(B) inflows to reach $80 billion at the close of the window

🚗Who foots the bill for damage from contaminated E20 fuel?

As E20 fuel (80 percent petrol blended with 20 percent ethanol) becomes the default at fuel outlets across the country, concerns on long-term impact of the mix on car engines and high risk of contaminated fuel have sparked a debate on who is liable for damage to the vehicle. While vehicle insurers have washed their hands off the liability, government might consider giving owners of vehicles manufactured before April 2023 the option of using E10 petrol.
