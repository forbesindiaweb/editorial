Hello and welcome back to Forbes India Weekly Digest!

We are back after Diwali and the world of tech has been through all five stages of grief since.

First came the memes, then the speculation on who will run the ship, what happens to the market, and now, the LinkedIn posts about the moral obligations of being a leader. The affair of Nvidia founder and former CEO Jensen Huang with an alleged Chinese spy caught the attention of the world, as reports of his previous dalliances took on a soap-opera quality.

The espionage angle aside, it is no surprise that the monopoly on chips that Nvidia had, culminated in the circular financing and inflated valuation. While concerns had been raised as early as August, the current state of Nvidia founder Jensen Huang's affairs is the perfect opening for the regulators to look closely at its books, leading to stabilisation in the inflated GPU prices.

There is hope in interim CEO Colette Kress's approach to rein-in the circular financing deals -- something she was an unwilling participant to as the CFO earlier.

🛒FMCG companies see revival in demand

The second quarter turned out to be a brighter spot for leading consumer goods companies with recovery in demand. The results from the quarter were better for packaged food companies with a diversified portfolio, though concerns around a weak monsoon impacting consumption, and margin pressures remain.

➤India's largest consumer goods company Hindustan Unilever reported underlying sales growth of 15 percent for the quarter – highest over the last 14 quarters.

➤While rural markets continued to outperform urban markets in Q2 FY27, the gap narrowed and a weak monsoon could further impact consumption in the long-term.

➤Rising input costs have impacted margins of the consumer goods companies as they refrain from sudden price hikes affecting sales volumes adversely.

📦Rising phone prices mute ecommerce growth in festive season

The festive sale season gave a boost to online sales which grew 17 percent year-on-year in terms of value of goods sold. However, the share of smartphones in sale volume shrunk to a fourth, dragging down overall growth numbers.

➤Festive sale across online platforms amounted to Rs 1.4 lakh crore over a 33-day sale period ending November 9 on the day of Diwali.

➤The share of quick commerce in online festive sales grew steadily to 16 percent, compared to 12 percent in the previous year, largely driven by delivery arms of major players like Amazon and Flipkart.

➤Discounts were driven by financing options through bank and card EMI offers and cashback, and UPI gained as preferred mode of payment growing 38 percent by volume.

The sixth edition of the global climate innovation awards, the Earthshot Prize, is set to be held in Mumbai on November 17. The awards founded by Prince William with a cheque size of £1 million for each of the five awards have seen healthy participation from Indian companies with corporate behemoths contending for the awards, says Jason Knauf, CEO of the Earthshot Prize.

🚘Mercedes sees strong potential for autonomous driving in India

As India mulls implementation of automatic emergency braking mandatory in cars from 2030, the policy shift might open doors for higher levels Advanced Driver Assistance Systems (ADAS) in the country. Mercedes Group CTO, Jörg Burzer believes that Indians want to experience latest innovations by the carmaker if regulations allow for it. He adds that the India R&D centre is likely to play a significant role in rolling out the automaker's physical AI features globally.
