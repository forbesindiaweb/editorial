Hello and welcome back to Forbes India Weekly Digest!

Tudum! And that marks the beginning of a major shake-up at Netflix as the streaming platform grapples with historic deceleration in revenue and decline in viewership.

This has resulted in heads rolling, with long time Chief Content Officer Bela Bajaria being replaced by Peter Rice, as well as the departure of head of film, Dan Lin. Overall, the platform laid off 1000 employees across product, tech, HR, and legal.

Netflix reported its slowest revenue growth in years last week as part of the Q3 earnings at $12.9 billion or a 12 percent increase year-on-year. According to reports, the average annualised revenue growth rate at Netflix has been close to 19.6 percent.

The streaming platform has also seen a stagnation in new subscriber numbers as customers downgrade their subscriptions amid password-sharing crackdowns by Netflix and rising inflation. The company's ad-supported tier has been slow to move, forcing it to downgrade projections from ad revenue from $3 billion to $2.7 billion for the year.

Interestingly, it is doing everything it said wouldn't, to stay relevant in the face of rising competition from YouTube and Skydance – the combined entity formed by Paramount, Warner Bros, and Discovery. Netflix's bids for live sports, video games, podcasts and other formats are yet to show concrete RoI, making investors jittery.

It remains to be seen whether the new leadership can work its magic to woo back the audience from free platforms and increase viewership with new content licensing deals.

🚀Levelling the playing field for India’s space tech companies

Indian National Space Promotion and Authorisation Centre (IN-SPACe) has served a key-role as a facilitator and interface between the private sector in Indian space tech and government space research organisation, ISRO. In an interview, IN-SPACe chairman Pawan Goenka says that the role of the organisation is not to monitor which Indian company – private or otherwise – manages to capture the global demand for small satellite launch vehicles; the idea is to make technology and infrastructure available to all in an open market to encourage the sector.

💰Management and succession planning at TVS

Leadership roles and responsibilities within a family business cannot substitute the fair division of wealth and assets – a point underscored by Mallika Srinivasan and her daughter, Lakshmi Venu, who have contested the claim that the division of assets within the TVS Group have been settled. The duo issued a statement saying that there has been no clear valuation and discussion on division of family assets of the larger TVS Group, including Amalgamations Group, which owns TAFE, of which Mallika serves as the chairperson and managing director.

💉Diabetes treatment moves from medicine to management

India ranks high globally in the number of diabetics, second only to China with 148 million diabetics in the country as of 2024. This makes the market an attractive target for pharmaceutical companies coming up with new treatments to manage the disease.

➤There has been a shift in diabetes treatment, from treating it in isolation, to treating it as part of overall lifestyle-related diseases.

➤Apart from the entry of semaglutides in the market, and Novo Nordisk's weekly-once insulin shot Awiqly, Indian firms and startups are also producing insulin pumps and constant glucose monitors.

➤Diabetes treatment today is individualised around cardiovascular risk, kidney disease, obesity, hypoglycaemia, diabetes technology and patient preference.

📈Investors want a piece of Jio’s digital ecosystem play

The Reliance JIO Platforms IPO which is set to list on Wednesday on the exchanges, saw high interest from investors looking to bet on the company's digital ecosystem expansion. The company offers more than a mere telco business with its foray into AI, cloud, and 5G.

➤The Rs 30,200 crore offering is an entirely fresh issue with Rs 27,500 crore earmarked to pare debts of Reliance Jio Infocomm, and the rest used to support future growth and corporate requirements.

➤JIO Platform's future growth will be supported by higher data consumption, home broadband expansion, and increasing adoption of cloud and digital solutions, believe industry analysts.

➤The company might also be able to leverage monetisation from AI-led services, digital applications, satellite connectivity, and overseas deployment of proprietary technology.

📲Freshworks reinvents itself as an AI-services company

A combination of acquisitions, streamlining products and a change in business strategy have defined the trajectory of Freshworks CEO Dennis Woodside who stepped into the role at the Nasdaq listed SaaS company in 2004. In the era of AI, software-as-a-service will continue to be bundled with it, believes the executive.

➤Freshworks has stopped smaller product offerings in favour of its suite of Freshservice offering, in its quest to be a multi-billion-dollar company.

➤Customers want to buy a broad set of IT capabilities, as a combination of software and AI allowing them to detect anomalies before it becomes a problem.

➤Freshworks' free cash flow guidance for the current financial year stands at $200 million, as it explores mixed-model pricing for its AI offerings.
