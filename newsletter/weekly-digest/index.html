One bill to amend them all is up for discussion in Rajya Sabha today, after the lower house cleared it on August 6. The Taxation and Other Laws (Amendment) Bill, 2026 proposes a series of tax changes spanning the Payment and Settlement Systems Act 2007, Income Tax Act 2025, and Finance Act 2026.

The taxation amendments under discussion aim to encourage foreign capital inflow and to mitigate the geopolitical shocks due to the ongoing war in West Asia.

The various amendments in the bill are aimed at

➤Simplifying eligibility criteria for offshore investment funds managed from India and tax exemptions on global income

➤Removing restrictions around eligibility and corpus, to make India an attractive destination for fund-management

➤Encouraging domestic electronics manufacturing sector through tax concessions

➤Relaxation of approval and notification requirements for foreign companies using Indian data centres and allowing data centres to operate through leased infrastructure

➤Strengthening India’s diamond trade industry through tax exemption for various eligible foreign stakeholders across value chain, including auction entities selling rough diamonds through notified special zones

➤Opening UPI transactions to Merchant Discount Rate (MDR) levies to allow banks and payment companies to charge a fee on UPI payments

While these are some salient takeaways, the impact of forward-looking measures around data centres, electronics manufacturing, and offshore investment entities will take time to play out and ease the burden on the economy.

🍹FSSAI cracks down on misleading food labels

The Food Safety and Standards Authority of India (FSSAI) has changed its approach from treating labelling and marketing norms as advisory to enforcing them to mean what they claim. The autonomous government body has stepped up scrutiny on claims made by consumer brands to ensure it is not misleading.

➤FSSAI has recently hauled up brands for using labels and marketing terms like ‘energy drink’, ‘organic’, ‘natural’, ‘healthy’, ‘freshly made’ and ‘no-added sugar.’

➤The regulatory action comes on the back of rapidly expanding premium food market since the pandemic.

➤In early 2025, FSSAI had fixed July 1 as the annual enforcement date for amendments via the Food Safety and Standards (Labelling and Display) Regulation, 2020.

⛽Boosting domestic exploration is key to combat global oil shocks

India managed to weather the impact on its crude oil imports resulting from the closure of Strait of Hormuz rather well, despite rising tensions between Tehran and Washington. The trend was observed globally as well, with countries managing inventory drawdowns and paring the demand, said Nick Sharma, executive director of upstream energy at S&P Global Energy.

➤India is going to be a key demand driver for oil for the next two decades to support its 6-8 percent GDP growth.

➤In order to secure India’s oil futures, domestic exploration activities by national oil companies will have to be coupled with taking a large equity position in overseas assets to reduce impact of open markets.

➤Continuing to build strategic relationship with Middle East is going to be the third driver in securing India’s position.

🎩Trump’s latest move to restrict birthright citizenship

US President Donald Trump is back at restricting birthright citizenship in US as part of his immigration agenda. Last week he signed two executive orders to restrict birthright citizenship for children born to immigrants in the country, including those on work or student visa. It is also aimed at curbing birth tourism to the country. However, the US Supreme Court had already struck down his earlier attempts at restricting the move.
